FEA · MECH 303 Machine Design · Project 2
Deep-Sea Pressure Hull — Structural Integrity Analysis
Inspired by the OceanGate Titan incident, this project evaluates the structural integrity of a deep-sea submersible pressure hull composed of Carbon Fiber Composite cylinder and Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) hemispherical endcaps using ANSYS Workbench at depths of 2,000–4,500 m.

Introduction

Deep-sea exploration requires robust structural designs capable of withstanding extreme hydrostatic pressures. The scope includes static structural analysis at depths from 2,000 m to 4,500 m, a parametric study on wall thickness (40–100 mm), and an elasto-plastic analysis to identify yield pressure and residual plastic strains.

Key Results

Hoop stress was approximately twice the axial stress, consistent with pressure vessel theory. At 3,800 m depth, minimum safety factor: cylinder 2.507, cap 3.74. Elasto-plastic analysis with Bilinear Isotropic Hardening identified yield pressure and residual strains after unloading.

FEA · MECH 303 Machine Design · Project 3
Bolted Joint — FE Modeling & Optimization
Comprehensive structural analysis of a bolted lap joint in ANSYS Mechanical, investigating bolt pretension, contact nonlinearity, and tensile loading to determine failure limits.

Analysis Phases

Task 1: 25 kN external load — stress concentrations at bolt head fillet confirmed, adequate contact pressure verified.

Task 2: Separation force determined at ~45.53 kN through incremental loading. Linear decay in contact pressure validated with non-linear stiffness response after separation.

Task 3: Optimization for 6 kN service load — Metric Class 5.8 bolt selected, preload 15,000 N, Safety Factor ≥ 1.3 against yielding and separation.

CFD · MECH 301 Fluid Mechanics · CFD Project I
Flow Through an Expansion in a Circular Pipe
Flow separation and reattachment in a sudden pipe expansion using ANSYS Fluent. Validated against Macagno & Hung (1967) and Hammad et al. (1999) experimental data.

Introduction

Understanding flow separation and predicting reattachment length optimizes system efficiency and minimizes pressure losses in piping networks, HVAC, and chemical reactors.

Laminar verification with grid convergence validated against Poiseuille solutions. Turbulent flow (Re = 10⁵) compared Standard k-ε and k-ω RANS models. Pressure loss coefficients evaluated across expansion ratios.

CFD · MECH 301 Fluid Mechanics · CFD Project II
Mixing Elbow — Flow & Heat Transfer Analysis
Two fluid streams at different temperatures in a 90° mixing elbow. Coupled mass, momentum, and energy equations solved in ANSYS Fluent (2D & 3D).

Key Findings

Laminar (Re=400): convection-dominated thermal stratification. Turbulent: Standard k-ε and SST k-ω compared with consistent velocity profiles and proven grid independence.

Parametric inlet velocity study showed higher jet momentum improves mixing. 3D analysis captured Dean vortices from centrifugal forces — critical lateral mixing not seen in 2D.

MATLAB · MECH 307 Engineering Dynamics
Moving & Bouncing Balls — Multi-Body Simulation
MATLAB simulation of elastic collision dynamics of multiple particles in a 2D domain.

Objectives

Model elastic collisions using Newton's Laws and Conservation of Linear Momentum (Hibbeler Ch. 15). Translate theoretical kinetics into a numerical algorithm. Validate through real-time energy conservation graphs.

Methodology

Momentum equations for particles A and B with impulse J. Coefficient of Restitution (e) relates pre/post-impact velocities. Substitution into restitution constraint solves for J analytically.
